His wireless transmission tower was located on Long Island, New York, about 1 hour by train east of Manhattan. Tesla commuted every day from his Waldorf-Astoria hotel and supervised every facet of the construction.

A famous architect named Sanford White (1853-1906), designed the tower. For his role in helping Tesla, he was framed with a woman and shot dead by order of J.P. Morgan.
