MODEL 01

AC induction motor

Simplified two-phase teaching model. It is not an engineering simulator.

TWO-PHASE VIEWphase = 0°
READOUT
Field speed3,000RPM
Rotor speed2,894RPM
Slip3.5%

The field moves slightly faster than the rotor. That difference induces rotor current and creates torque.

field speed60 × frequency
────────────
pole pairs
Model assumptions and source

The model preserves the relationships among supply frequency, pole pairs, synchronous speed, rotor speed and slip. It omits winding distribution, resistance, reactance, saturation and mechanical losses. The rotating-field explanation is grounded in Tesla’s 1888 AIEE paper as reprinted in Thomas Commerford Martin’s 1894 collection.
